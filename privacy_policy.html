Privacy Policy for H-Fire (Resident Monitor)

Last Updated: April 09, 2026

1. Introduction

H-Fire ("we," "our," or "us") provides a Fire and Gas Leak Monitoring System. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate safety monitoring and emergency response dispatch, we collect the following Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

  • Account Information: Email address, Full Name (First, Middle, and Last Name) via authentication providers.
  • Location Data: Geographic Location (Latitude and Longitude) and detailed Household Address / Community / Block & Lot information. This is strictly required to locate the household during fire or hazardous gas leak events.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Sensor unit telemetry (Gas PPM levels, flame detection states, node battery/connection health).
  • Identifiers: Unique User ID (userId) and Expo push notification device tokens.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for safety and life-saving monitoring purposes:

  • Account Authentication: Securely authenticate and maintain user accounts.
  • Emergency Monitoring & Alerts: Track gas and smoke concentrations in real time and send high-priority push notifications and alarm sirens during dangerous conditions.
  • Emergency Response Dispatch: Allow designated administrators and first responders to pinpoint the exact location of active hazards.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers to power core functionality:

  • Clerk: Secure user authentication and session management.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database storage with row-level security (RLS) for profile and incident records.
  • HiveMQ: MQTT cloud broker for real-time IoT hardware communication.
  • Expo & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Real-time push notification delivery for emergency alarms.

5. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS, secure WebSockets) to protect your data in transit and at rest. Your household location and profile data are stored securely and accessible only by authorized administrators during active hazard events.

6. Data Deletion Instructions

Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • Send an email to: hfire.iotsyste@gmail.com
  • Subject line: "H-Fire Data Deletion Request"
  • Include your registered email address in the body of the message.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your user profile, location data, and credentials within 48 hours.
